Wi-Fi and printers

Staff Wi-Fi: connect to "Northwind-Staff" and sign in with your company account. Guests use "Northwind-Guest"; reception has the weekly guest code.

Printers are next to each pantry. Print jobs are held until you tap your badge on the printer, so nothing confidential sits in the tray.

If Wi-Fi or a printer stops working, raise an IT ticket from the help portal or message #it-help.
