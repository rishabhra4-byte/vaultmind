# Office Guide: Bengaluru

The office is on the 4th floor of Prestige Tech Park, Outer Ring Road. Doors open at 8am and close at 9pm on weekdays.

Visitors sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge. Host your visitor in a meeting room, not at your desk.

Meeting rooms are booked in the calendar. Rooms on the east side seat four; Banyan and Peepal seat twelve and have video conferencing.

Parking is on levels B1 and B2. Bicycle racks are next to the main entrance. The cafeteria serves lunch from 12:30 to 2:30.
