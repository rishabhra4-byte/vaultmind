# Interview Scorecard Template

Use one scorecard per interview, and submit it within 24 hours so the hiring panel can meet quickly.

Rate each skill from 1 (no evidence) to 4 (strong evidence): problem solving, communication, role knowledge and collaboration.

Write what the candidate said or did, not your impression of them. Don't discuss the candidate with other interviewers before you've submitted your own scorecard.

Overall recommendation: strong hire, hire, no hire or strong no hire. Explain any "strong" rating in one or two sentences.
