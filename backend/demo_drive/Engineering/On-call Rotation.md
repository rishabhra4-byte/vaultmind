# On-call Rotation

Each backend engineer joins the on-call rotation after their first three months. A shift is one week, Monday 10am to Monday 10am.

The primary on-call responds to pages within 15 minutes, day or night. The secondary takes over if the primary doesn't acknowledge within 30 minutes.

Hand over at the Monday stand-up: open incidents, anything flaky, and planned releases for the week.

On-call engineers get one day off in lieu for every week of on-call. Swap shifts in the #oncall channel and update the rota.
